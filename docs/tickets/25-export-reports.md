# 25 — Export reports as Excel or PDF

**What to build:** the owner opens Reports from the Analytics tab, picks
a month, and exports that month's report as an Excel workbook or a PDF.

**Blocked by:** 10

**Status:** todo

- [ ] Analytics has a "Reports" button, for the owner only; staff who
      type the address go back to the Workspace
- [ ] A month picker, defaulting to this month (Manila time)
- [ ] The report shows: received, picked up, cancelled, still open,
      earned, average turnaround; by payment method; by status; and
      every job received that month (date, device, problem, customer,
      status, quote, revised, paid, method, handled by)
- [ ] No customer phone numbers anywhere in a report
- [ ] Export Excel downloads an .xlsx with a Summary sheet and a Jobs
      sheet, with peso and date formatting; built in the browser,
      nothing sent to any outside service
- [ ] Export PDF opens the device's print screen ("Save as PDF"), in
      landscape, without the top bar or buttons
- [ ] The report's numbers match the Analytics tab for the same month
- [ ] Works on the live URL
