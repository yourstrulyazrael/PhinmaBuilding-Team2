# 30 — Retail price, and parts in the analytics

**What to build:** each part has a cost (what the shop pays, owner only)
and a retail price (what the customer is charged). The prices are
recorded when a part is used, and Analytics and Reports show parts sold,
parts cost and parts profit for the chosen period.
